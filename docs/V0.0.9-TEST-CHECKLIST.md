# Crafting Core v0.0.9 — Live Test Checklist

Target: Foundry VTT 14.365 + D&D5e 5.3.3.

## 1. Upgrade / catalog sync

- Install v0.0.9 over the current v0.0.8 test world.
- Open **Materials** and run **Create / Sync Materials** once.
- Confirm the built-in catalog reports **132** curated materials.
- Confirm an **Essences** folder exists in `Crafting Core — Materials`.
- Confirm these Uncommon materials exist: Arcane, Acid, Cold, Flame, Force, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic, Radiant, Thunder Essence.
- Confirm the existing **Necrotic Essence** material has moved into the new Essences family without changing its stable material ID.
- Confirm a new **Necrotic Residue** Uncommon material exists in the Undead family to preserve the physical/thematic four-slot pool.

## 2. Existing-profile safety

- Before rescanning, open one Harvest Profile created in v0.0.8.
- Confirm its four automatic slots and Pinpoint Overrides are unchanged.
- Confirm Slot 5 reports that Essence analysis is pending reanalysis rather than inventing an affinity.

## 3. Actor with no non-physical affinity

- Reanalyze a mundane Beast/Monstrosity whose non-spell attacks are physical and which has no elemental/energy resistance or immunity.
- Confirm the editor shows **Slot 5 — Essence** with `50% Arcane Essence / 50% No Essence`.
- Generate repeated Harvest previews and confirm Arcane Essence appears roughly half the time over a large sample.

## 4. Single-affinity Actor

- Reanalyze an Actor with a clear non-spell Fire, Cold, Lightning, Necrotic, Radiant, Poison, Psychic, Force, Thunder, or Acid damage Activity and/or matching resistance/immunity.
- Confirm the detected affinity appears in the Harvest Profile Editor.
- Confirm Slot 5 shows `45% Arcane Essence / 55% Specific Essence`.
- Generate repeated Harvest previews and confirm both Arcane and the matching Essence appear, with the specific Essence slightly more common over a large sample.

## 5. Multiple affinities

- Reanalyze an Actor with at least two supported non-physical damage affinities.
- Confirm both are listed.
- Generate many Harvests and confirm the specific 55% side is distributed between those types rather than creating an extra Essence slot.
- Confirm only one Essence maximum is produced per harvested source.

## 6. False-positive regressions

- Reanalyze an Actor with only bludgeoning/piercing/slashing attacks: these must not become Essence affinities.
- Give or inspect an Actor with a prepared elemental spell but no matching non-spell damage/resistance/immunity: the spell alone must not establish that affinity.
- Confirm ordinary four-slot anatomy inference remains unchanged from v0.0.8.

## 7. Pinpoint regression

- Add or keep a Pinpoint Override on an Actor.
- Reanalyze.
- Confirm the Pinpoint is preserved and remains outside all five automatic slots.
- Generate Harvest and confirm the Pinpoint roll and the Essence roll can both occur in the same result.

## 8. Scanner / UI

- Run a configured Compendium scan.
- Confirm scanned profiles display an Essence indicator alongside automatic-slot and Pinpoint counts.
- Search for an Essence damage type such as `fire` or `necrotic` and confirm matching profiles can be found.
- Open several profiles and confirm the Essence inference reason is readable.

## 9. Regression

- Generate Environment Gathering and confirm Biome generation is unchanged.
- Generate manual Creature Harvest by Creature Type and confirm its existing coarse-profile behavior is unchanged.
- Learn and craft one existing Recipe to confirm the crafting core remains unaffected.
